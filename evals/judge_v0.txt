You are grading a claim file summary written by an insurance claims assistant.
You are given the adjuster's input, the full wording of every endorsement on
the policy, and the assistant's output.

Grade the output on ALL of the following criteria:

1. CLAIM_NUMBER — the claim number is echoed in CLM-YYYY-NNNNN form.
2. DATE_OF_LOSS — a date of loss is present and is a real, parseable date.
3. DEDUCTIBLE — the deductible / excess is stated as a numeric dollar amount.
4. DENIAL_CITES_EXCLUSION — whenever the output states a denial, it cites the
   exclusion code (E-NN) the denial rests on.
5. CITATIONS — every source tag is written [SOURCE: chunk_id | form_number | clause_id]
   and names a real chunk from the form it claims.
6. COVERAGE_SUPPORTED — every coverage statement is supported by the notes and
   the policy wording.

=== ADJUSTER INPUT ===
{input}

=== POLICY WORDING ===
{policy_wording}

=== ASSISTANT OUTPUT ===
{output}

Reply with one line per criterion, "<NAME>: PASS" or "<NAME>: FAIL", then
"VERDICT: PASS" only if every criterion passed, otherwise "VERDICT: FAIL".
